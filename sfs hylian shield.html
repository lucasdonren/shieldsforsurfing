Hylian Shield

About this Product


The Hylian shield, once commonly found in the land of Hyrule, now a relic of the hero Link. This shield is the strongest of the strongest, owned by generations of heroes and left without a scratch. This shield seems to be imbued with the power of the triforce of courage. It is lightweight, sturdy, and fireproof, and its metallic material seems to make it near unbreakable. The wooden interior allows for comfort while using, and the blue design with the golden triforce and red Hylian crest makes it a highly sought after item for enthusiasts of shield surfing.
