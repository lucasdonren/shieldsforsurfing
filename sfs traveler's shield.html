Traveler's Shield

About this Product


The Traveler's shield, our most cost effective option. Perfect for those who need something sturdy and effective without the high price point. This shield, professionally crafted with Hateno wood, has a circular shape that allows smooth travel over snow and sand. Its steel edge adds to its durability, allowing the shield to last for as long as possible. Just be careful about going near fire, as this shield is unfortunate flammable. The design on the front of the shield is handpainted by our artisans, bearing the design of dragons.
