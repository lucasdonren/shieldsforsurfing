Ever wanted to travel, but walking is too tiring and a horse is too much hassle?
Then we have the solution for you.

Our patented shield surfing allows for fast and smooth travel without the necessity of an animal. Simply hop on one of our shields, catch your balance, and ride to your heart's content. Our shields come in many shapes, sizes, materials and designs in order to fit not only your surfing needs but also your fashion. Do you prefer something sleek and elegant? Perhaps the Royal shield is for you. Maybe something stylish and flashy? The Kite shield. Shield surfing is practical, inexpensive, and only has a mild risk of death. As long as you don't break your shield while riding it, we're sure that you'll be just fine.
