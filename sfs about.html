Frequently asked questions:


Q: How long will my shield last before I'll have to replace it?
A: Depending on which product you choose it mey last you your entire life or for only one ride. No refunds.


Q: I've seen people using shields for surfing while gliding. Is this an approved usage?
A: Do not attempt. Yes, one of our clients is capable of pulling this off, but that does not mean that it is safe for all users.


Q: Why does the Knight's shield come with scratches already on it? Is this a shipping issue?
A: This is not a shipping issue, the scratches on the Knight's shield are a sign of its authenticity. Of course a shield used by one of the knights of Hyrule would have some minor damage, they're still perfectly useable.


Q: Where do you source your shields?
A: We get our shields directly from the ruins of Hyrule, collected by professionals. There is no need to worry for their safety, they know what they're doing.


Q: Will you get more shields in stock? The selection is slim.
A: If we get enough sales, then of course we will expand our stock. Perhaps we could get into the market of monster shields, Lynel shields are especially strong.
