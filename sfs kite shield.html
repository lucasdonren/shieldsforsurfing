Kite Shield

About this Product


The Kite shield, specially designed for surfing in the thick snow of the Tabantha region. Made with traditional Rito style, this shield has the emblem of the Rito engraved on it and feathered tassles coming off the end, guaranteeing a stylish look as you carry it with you. The shield is shaped specially to carve through the snow, and its light weight allows a perfect gliding motion.

Information
Sizing: 60cm height, 50cm width
Materials: Tabantha wood, Rito feathers
Special: Cutting and gliding through thick snow, specially collected feathers
